rclone-cajun Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

rclone-cajun accesses only its owner's own Google Drive data, through Google's Drive API, from the owner's own computer.

No data is collected, sold, shared, or sent to any third party. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer only.

rclone-cajun's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
